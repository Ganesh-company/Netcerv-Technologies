Do you provide remote and onsite support?

Yes. Remote or onsite support can be discussed depending on the issue, your location and what is practical for the job.

NetCerv Technologies serves Johor Bahru and surrounding areas. Contact us to discuss your location and support needs.

Yes. Support can include device setup, software and Windows installation, updates, connectivity checks and related provisioning tasks.

Yes. We can discuss Wi-Fi and internet troubleshooting, router setup, network connectivity, printer installation and common peripheral issues.

Do you support Microsoft 365?

Yes. Assistance is available for Microsoft 365 setup and everyday tools such as email, Teams and SharePoint.

Send us a WhatsApp message describing the issue and your preferred support arrangement. We can discuss the next steps with you.
